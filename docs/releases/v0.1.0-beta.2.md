A tidier Divit: the same features, less on screen.

**What changed**

- **Shorter toolbar.** Mode, Research and Export stay in view. Underlines, Snapshots, Start a rewrite and Archive are under **More**.
- **Research beside your text.** On a wide window the Research panel sits next to the page and your text re-flows, instead of being covered. On smaller windows it still slides over, with a clear close button.
- **One quiet line under the title.** Place, date, language and tags fold into a single line; click it to edit them.
- **Calmer sidebar.** Search sits right under **New writing**. New story or book and Bring in writing are in the small menu beside it (Ctrl Shift N and Ctrl O still work).
- **Settings in tabs.** General, Writing, Appearance, Your files, AI and About. Save and Cancel are always visible, and Save lights up as soon as you change something.
- **Simpler AI settings.** One short sentence per field; prices and privacy details are under **Details**.
- **Clearer spelling suggestions.** Each card shows the word, the suggestions as buttons, and Ignore once / Ignore always / Add to dictionary.
- **Easier-to-read labels.** Small labels are larger now and keep good contrast in every theme.
- **Clearer tab names.** The side panel tabs are now Notebook (project notes and writing log) and Reflection (a few questions to ask your draft).
- **Simpler wordmark.** The dot after "Divit" is gone from the header and the icon.

**Known limitations**

- The installer is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn. Choose More info → Run anyway.
- Research, automatic backups and Publish to my site still need [Node.js 22](https://nodejs.org).
- Some messages from the app's core stay in English with the Turkish interface.

Please report problems with **Help → Report a bug**, or at https://github.com/illfollowthesun/Divit/issues.
